The Stork is Aroused

Historical image match: unverified. Modern teaching may vary.

Position and movement

Ewers describes four actions: the first two on one leg, then a return to ground contact and a forward step. The raised foot extends before returning.

Keith Ewers · The Stork Is Aroused · Later teaching

Application study

Ewers assigns receiving, ward-off and other force relationships to both the arms and the lifted leg. This is an interpretation of the whole moving sequence.
