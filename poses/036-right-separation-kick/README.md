# 36. Right Separation Kick

Historical image study. Modern teaching may differ as the art evolves. Names and numbers are preserved from [moves.md](../../moves.md); notes are editorial observations and interpretations, not a reconstruction of every historical instruction.

## Identification

**direct** — Preparation and right separation kick are separate panels.

## Position

The right leg lifts and extends while the hands open apart.

## Transition

Read the preparation and kick as separate phases; the montage supplies both here.

Preceded by: High Pat on Horse. Followed by: Left Separation Kick.

## Application study — interpretation

The hands and lifted leg suggest simultaneous upper contact and a lower action; exact targets remain interpretive.

## Breathing and attention — editorial suggestions

Breathing and attention are editorial practice suggestions, not recovered instructions for this particular photograph. Allow an easy, unforced breath; do not impose a fixed inhalation or exhalation on each numbered movement.

Notice balance on the standing leg instead of pursuing height.

## Name and context

Separation Kick is distinguished from Heel Kick in the supplied sequence.
