# 74. Step Back and Repulse the Monkey, Left

Historical image study. Modern teaching may differ as the art evolves. Names and numbers are preserved from [moves.md](../../moves.md); notes are editorial observations and interpretations, not a reconstruction of every historical instruction.

## Identification

**reused** — Third repetition shares the first left-side image of this series.

## Position

One hand reaches forward while the other withdraws; the sequence travels backwards.

## Transition

Compare consecutive side changes, not just the forward-facing palm.

Preceded by: Step Back and Repulse the Monkey, Right. Followed by: Diagonal Flying.

## Application study — interpretation

Retreat with maintained contact is a useful interpretive theme; withdrawing need not mean abandoning the forward line.

## Breathing and attention — editorial suggestions

Breathing and attention are editorial practice suggestions, not recovered instructions for this particular photograph. Allow an easy, unforced breath; do not impose a fixed inhalation or exhalation on each numbered movement.

Attend to the landing foot before transferring weight to it.

## Name and context

Side labels vary between sources: an advancing hand and a retreating leg can name the same phase differently.
