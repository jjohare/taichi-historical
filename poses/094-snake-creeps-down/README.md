# 94. Snake Creeps Down

Historical image study. Modern teaching may differ as the art evolves. Names and numbers are preserved from [moves.md](../../moves.md); notes are editorial observations and interpretations, not a reconstruction of every historical instruction.

## Identification

**direct** — Second low posture, leading to Seven Stars rather than Golden Rooster.

## Position

The body lowers into a long stance, with one arm reaching along the lower line.

## Transition

The first occurrence leads to Golden Rooster; the second leads to Seven Stars.

Preceded by: Single Whip. Followed by: Step Forward Seven Stars.

## Application study — interpretation

Changing level while maintaining contact is one possible interpretation of the low shape.

## Breathing and attention — editorial suggestions

Breathing and attention are editorial practice suggestions, not recovered instructions for this particular photograph. Allow an easy, unforced breath; do not impose a fixed inhalation or exhalation on each numbered movement.

Use the photograph to observe relationships, not to demand its depth from your own joints.

## Name and context

The same low posture has different continuations at its two locations in the sequence.
