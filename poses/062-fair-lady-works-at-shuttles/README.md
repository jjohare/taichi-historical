# 62. Fair Lady Works at Shuttles

Historical image study. Modern teaching may differ as the art evolves. Names and numbers are preserved from [moves.md](../../moves.md); notes are editorial observations and interpretations, not a reconstruction of every historical instruction.

## Identification

**direct** — Four corners belong to one entry in moves.md. Preserve their order and the changing camera-relative directions.

## Position

One forearm rises above the head while the other hand extends; four plates show changing corners.

## Transition

Follow the four images in order. They belong to one numbered entry in the supplied list.

Preceded by: Single Whip. Followed by: Grasp the Sparrow's tail.

## Application study — interpretation

Upper receiving and forward contact offer an interpretive theme across changes of direction.

## Breathing and attention — editorial suggestions

Breathing and attention are editorial practice suggestions, not recovered instructions for this particular photograph. Allow an easy, unforced breath; do not impose a fixed inhalation or exhalation on each numbered movement.

Re-establish the feet and gaze at each corner instead of following only the hands.

## Name and context

The weaving title is retained; this catalogue does not infer a literal loom mechanism from the name.
