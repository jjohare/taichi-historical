# 56. Diagonal Single Whip

Historical image study. Modern teaching may differ as the art evolves. Names and numbers are preserved from [moves.md](../../moves.md); notes are editorial observations and interpretations, not a reconstruction of every historical instruction.

## Identification

**direct** — Local and official numbering agree from here onwards. Diagonal Single Whip is a different orientation, not an extra entry to insert before it.

## Position

The familiar hook and open palm appear on a different line through the floor.

## Transition

Read the changed direction in relation to the preceding Embrace the Tiger and the following Parting Mane.

Preceded by: Embrace the Tiger and Return to Mountain. Followed by: Parting Wild Horse's Mane, Right.

## Application study — interpretation

Explore the same two-arm relationship on a diagonal rather than assuming a new hand technique.

## Breathing and attention — editorial suggestions

Breathing and attention are editorial practice suggestions, not recovered instructions for this particular photograph. Allow an easy, unforced breath; do not impose a fixed inhalation or exhalation on each numbered movement.

Track the direction of the feet as well as that of the extended palm.

## Name and context

Diagonal identifies this occurrence in the supplied modern sequence.
