# 102. Closing

Historical image study. Modern teaching may differ as the art evolves. Names and numbers are preserved from [moves.md](../../moves.md); notes are editorial observations and interpretations, not a reconstruction of every historical instruction.

## Identification

**endpoint-reference** — The standing final panel is a shared endpoint, not a separate picture of the arms descending. Chen reuses his opening photograph for the close.

## Position

The montage preserves a standing endpoint rather than a separate view of the lowering arms.

## Transition

Read Closing after the final Cross Hands, which is also omitted as a distinct final montage frame.

Preceded by: Cross Hands. Followed by: Return to Wu Chi.

## Application study — interpretation

Study the return from an active shape to resting alignment rather than assigning a speculative combat use.

## Breathing and attention — editorial suggestions

Breathing and attention are editorial practice suggestions, not recovered instructions for this particular photograph. Allow an easy, unforced breath; do not impose a fixed inhalation or exhalation on each numbered movement.

Let attention broaden as the hands settle.

## Name and context

Closing and Return share a final photograph; they are still separate entries in the supplied list.
