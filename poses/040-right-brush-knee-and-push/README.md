# 40. Right Brush Knee and Push

Historical image study. Modern teaching may differ as the art evolves. Names and numbers are preserved from [moves.md](../../moves.md); notes are editorial observations and interpretations, not a reconstruction of every historical instruction.

## Identification

**direct** — Right Brush Knee before the low punch.

## Position

The right hand is low beside the leading knee while the opposite palm reaches forward.

## Transition

Compare this side with the neighbouring left version, allowing for the changed camera view.

Preceded by: Left Brush Knee and Push. Followed by: Step Forward and Punch Down.

## Application study — interpretation

Study the relationship between clearing a low line and extending the opposite palm.

## Breathing and attention — editorial suggestions

Breathing and attention are editorial practice suggestions, not recovered instructions for this particular photograph. Allow an easy, unforced breath; do not impose a fixed inhalation or exhalation on each numbered movement.

Keep awareness of both feet during the side change.

## Name and context

A rear view of the right version is not evidence that the source image was mirrored.
