# 80. Fan Through the Back

Historical image study. Modern teaching may differ as the art evolves. Names and numbers are preserved from [moves.md](../../moves.md); notes are editorial observations and interpretations, not a reconstruction of every historical instruction.

## Identification

**direct** — Second Fan Through the Back.

## Position

One hand extends forward while the other shelters high beside the head.

## Transition

The source sequence places this opening after Needle and before a turning action.

Preceded by: Needle at Sea Bottom. Followed by: Turn Bodyand White Snake Spits out Tongue.

## Application study — interpretation

Study simultaneous protection above and extension ahead as an interpretation of the two-arm shape.

## Breathing and attention — editorial suggestions

Breathing and attention are editorial practice suggestions, not recovered instructions for this particular photograph. Allow an easy, unforced breath; do not impose a fixed inhalation or exhalation on each numbered movement.

Notice the width across the back without stiffening the shoulders.

## Name and context

Fan Through the Back is retained as supplied; English translations of this name differ.
