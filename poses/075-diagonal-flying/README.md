# 75. Diagonal Flying

Historical image study. Modern teaching may differ as the art evolves. Names and numbers are preserved from [moves.md](../../moves.md); notes are editorial observations and interpretations, not a reconstruction of every historical instruction.

## Identification

**direct** — Diagonal Flying after the second retreating series.

## Position

The arms open along a diagonal, with the trunk and stance turned from the preceding retreat.

## Transition

Observe how the outward arm and step develop together into a wider shape.

Preceded by: Step Back and Repulse the Monkey, Left. Followed by: Raise Hands and Step Forward.

## Application study — interpretation

An opening across a contact line is one possible reading; the photograph cannot determine the opponent's placement.

## Breathing and attention — editorial suggestions

Breathing and attention are editorial practice suggestions, not recovered instructions for this particular photograph. Allow an easy, unforced breath; do not impose a fixed inhalation or exhalation on each numbered movement.

Notice the connection across the back between the two arms.

## Name and context

Diagonal Flying is a posture title, not a claim that the body leaves the ground.
