# 57. Parting Wild Horse's Mane, Right

Historical image study. Modern teaching may differ as the art evolves. Names and numbers are preserved from [moves.md](../../moves.md); notes are editorial observations and interpretations, not a reconstruction of every historical instruction.

## Identification

**direct** — First right Parting Mane.

## Position

The arms separate diagonally as the right-leading stance opens.

## Transition

The alternating steps continue the same family; the montage reuses its right-side image for the third entry.

Preceded by: Diagonal Single Whip. Followed by: Parting Wild Horse's Mane, Left.

## Application study — interpretation

The separated arms suggest opening through or around a line of contact.

## Breathing and attention — editorial suggestions

Breathing and attention are editorial practice suggestions, not recovered instructions for this particular photograph. Allow an easy, unforced breath; do not impose a fixed inhalation or exhalation on each numbered movement.

Notice the lower hand as well as the more conspicuous raised arm.

## Name and context

The horse-mane title supplies imagery, but the archive does not establish an origin tale.
