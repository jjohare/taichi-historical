# 48. Twin Fists Strike Opponents Ears

Historical image study. Modern teaching may differ as the art evolves. Names and numbers are preserved from [moves.md](../../moves.md); notes are editorial observations and interpretations, not a reconstruction of every historical instruction.

## Identification

**direct** — Both fists curve towards the head-height target; this remains a solo illustration.

## Position

Both fists approach from the sides at approximately head height.

## Transition

The posture follows a heel kick; the landing and gathering are part of the transition, not shown by the endpoint alone.

Preceded by: Turn Body and Right Heel Kick. Followed by: Left Heel Kick.

## Application study — interpretation

The name suggests paired head-level strikes. The archive records solo form rather than a demonstration on a partner.

## Breathing and attention — editorial suggestions

Breathing and attention are editorial practice suggestions, not recovered instructions for this particular photograph. Allow an easy, unforced breath; do not impose a fixed inhalation or exhalation on each numbered movement.

Attend to the roundness of the arms without clenching the shoulders.

## Name and context

The explicit target belongs to the supplied historical-style name; it is not a training recommendation.
