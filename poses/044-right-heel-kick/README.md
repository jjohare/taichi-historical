# 44. Right Heel Kick

Historical image study. Modern teaching may differ as the art evolves. Names and numbers are preserved from [moves.md](../../moves.md); notes are editorial observations and interpretations, not a reconstruction of every historical instruction.

## Identification

**direct** — First right heel kick in the central kicking sequence.

## Position

The right leg extends with the heel presented and the arms separated.

## Transition

Check the preceding entry to distinguish this repeated endpoint from the different turns that lead to it.

Preceded by: Step Forward, Parry Block and Punch. Followed by: Left Strike Tiger.

## Application study — interpretation

Study a heel-led action with the arms maintaining spatial awareness.

## Breathing and attention — editorial suggestions

Breathing and attention are editorial practice suggestions, not recovered instructions for this particular photograph. Allow an easy, unforced breath; do not impose a fixed inhalation or exhalation on each numbered movement.

Notice unnecessary tension in the jaw and shoulders during single-leg balance.

## Name and context

Repeated right heel kicks are different occurrences of a related posture, not duplicate catalogue errors.
