# 4. Raise Hands and Step Forward

Historical image study. Modern teaching may differ as the art evolves. Names and numbers are preserved from [moves.md](../../moves.md); notes are editorial observations and interpretations, not a reconstruction of every historical instruction.

## Identification

**direct** — The terminal raised-hands position is shown; the preceding step is not a separate panel.

## Position

The hands face towards each other at different heights, with the forward foot lightly placed.

## Transition

Notice the narrowing of the stance after Single Whip and the subsequent opening into White Crane.

Preceded by: Single whip. Followed by: White Crane Spreads its Wings.

## Application study — interpretation

The converging arms suggest a study of receiving between two contact points; this is an interpretation of the shape.

## Breathing and attention — editorial suggestions

Breathing and attention are editorial practice suggestions, not recovered instructions for this particular photograph. Allow an easy, unforced breath; do not impose a fixed inhalation or exhalation on each numbered movement.

Avoid making the arms tense merely to hold their outline.

## Name and context

The name includes a step, although the montage records only its terminal position.
