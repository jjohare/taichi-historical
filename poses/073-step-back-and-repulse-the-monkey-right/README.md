# 73. Step Back and Repulse the Monkey, Right

Historical image study. Modern teaching may differ as the art evolves. Names and numbers are preserved from [moves.md](../../moves.md); notes are editorial observations and interpretations, not a reconstruction of every historical instruction.

## Identification

**reused** — The opposite-side montage image is borrowed from the earlier series; the 1931 manual supplies its own later right-side photograph.

## Position

The opposite hand-and-foot arrangement continues the retreating series.

## Transition

Follow the alternation with the left-labelled occurrence while retaining the backward travel.

Preceded by: Step Back and Repulse the Monkey, Left. Followed by: Step Back and Repulse the Monkey, Left.

## Application study — interpretation

Study changing the receiving and returning roles of the hands during a retreat.

## Breathing and attention — editorial suggestions

Breathing and attention are editorial practice suggestions, not recovered instructions for this particular photograph. Allow an easy, unforced breath; do not impose a fixed inhalation or exhalation on each numbered movement.

Keep attention on the space behind the stepping foot as well as in front.

## Name and context

The repeated series is condensed in the montage; reused images are labelled explicitly.
