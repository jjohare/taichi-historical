# 89. High Pat on On Horse with Palm Thrust

Historical image study. Modern teaching may differ as the art evolves. Names and numbers are preserved from [moves.md](../../moves.md); notes are editorial observations and interpretations, not a reconstruction of every historical instruction.

## Identification

**direct** — The reaching palm is the distinguishing continuation; do not substitute the earlier High Pat endpoint alone.

## Position

The High Pat family continues into a further palm reach.

## Transition

Follow the continuation beyond the earlier High Pat endpoint before the Cross Kick.

Preceded by: Single Whip. Followed by: Cross Kick.

## Application study — interpretation

A reaching contact after gathering is a possible study of the extended action.

## Breathing and attention — editorial suggestions

Breathing and attention are editorial practice suggestions, not recovered instructions for this particular photograph. Allow an easy, unforced breath; do not impose a fixed inhalation or exhalation on each numbered movement.

Let the eyes follow the intended direction without pushing the head forwards.

## Name and context

The added Palm Thrust wording distinguishes this occurrence from the earlier High Pat.
