# 96. Step back and Ride the Tiger

Historical image study. Modern teaching may differ as the art evolves. Names and numbers are preserved from [moves.md](../../moves.md); notes are editorial observations and interpretations, not a reconstruction of every historical instruction.

## Identification

**direct** — Visually resembles White Crane. Its location after Seven Stars and before Lotus establishes this occurrence as Ride the Tiger.

## Position

One hand rises and the other lowers while the weight gathers back.

## Transition

Its place between Seven Stars and Lotus identifies this occurrence despite its resemblance to White Crane.

Preceded by: Step Forward Seven Stars. Followed by: Turn Body and Swing Over Lotus.

## Application study — interpretation

The contrast between upper and lower contact lines offers one interpretive study.

## Breathing and attention — editorial suggestions

Breathing and attention are editorial practice suggestions, not recovered instructions for this particular photograph. Allow an easy, unforced breath; do not impose a fixed inhalation or exhalation on each numbered movement.

Attend to the withdrawal through the feet rather than leaning the trunk back.

## Name and context

A similar silhouette does not make this the same named movement as White Crane.
