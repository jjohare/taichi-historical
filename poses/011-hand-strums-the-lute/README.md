# 11. Hand Strums the Lute

Historical image study. Modern teaching may differ as the art evolves. Names and numbers are preserved from [moves.md](../../moves.md); notes are editorial observations and interpretations, not a reconstruction of every historical instruction.

## Identification

**direct** — Second Lute occurrence.

## Position

Both hands gather in front, the forward foot light and the weight drawn back.

## Transition

The narrowing from Brush Knee is visible in the change of stance; the intermediate hand path is not fully recorded.

Preceded by: Left Brush Knee and Push. Followed by: Left Brush Knee and Push.

## Application study — interpretation

The two hands can be studied as coordinated contact points along an arm. This is an interpretive possibility.

## Breathing and attention — editorial suggestions

Breathing and attention are editorial practice suggestions, not recovered instructions for this particular photograph. Allow an easy, unforced breath; do not impose a fixed inhalation or exhalation on each numbered movement.

Notice the space between the hands without squeezing it shut.

## Name and context

The instrument name describes a recognisable arrangement; it is not evidence of a literal playing motion.
