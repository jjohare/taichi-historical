# 95. Step Forward Seven Stars

Historical image study. Modern teaching may differ as the art evolves. Names and numbers are preserved from [moves.md](../../moves.md); notes are editorial observations and interpretations, not a reconstruction of every historical instruction.

## Identification

**direct** — Crossed fists and a light front foot distinguish this from the bow-stance Punch.

## Position

The fists cross in front and the forward foot is lightly placed.

## Transition

The body rises from the low posture, then withdraws into Ride the Tiger.

Preceded by: Snake Creeps Down. Followed by: Step back and Ride the Tiger.

## Application study — interpretation

The crossed arms offer a study of gathering or receiving along two lines.

## Breathing and attention — editorial suggestions

Breathing and attention are editorial practice suggestions, not recovered instructions for this particular photograph. Allow an easy, unforced breath; do not impose a fixed inhalation or exhalation on each numbered movement.

Notice the change from the long low base to the smaller upright one.

## Name and context

Seven Stars is a traditional title; no unverified astronomical or anatomical explanation is assigned here.
