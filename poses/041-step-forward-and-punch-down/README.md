# 41. Step Forward and Punch Down

Historical image study. Modern teaching may differ as the art evolves. Names and numbers are preserved from [moves.md](../../moves.md); notes are editorial observations and interpretations, not a reconstruction of every historical instruction.

## Identification

**direct** — Downward punch; visually similar to the later groin punch, but a different sequence position.

## Position

The fist angles down from a forward stance and the trunk inclines with it.

## Transition

Compare the preceding Brush Knee and the following turn to locate this low punch.

Preceded by: Right Brush Knee and Push. Followed by: Turn Body and Chop with Fist.

## Application study — interpretation

A descending line of contact is visible; a particular target is not recoverable from the solo plate alone.

## Breathing and attention — editorial suggestions

Breathing and attention are editorial practice suggestions, not recovered instructions for this particular photograph. Allow an easy, unforced breath; do not impose a fixed inhalation or exhalation on each numbered movement.

Follow the diagonal through the whole body rather than folding only at the neck.

## Name and context

This entry remains separate from the later Punch Groin despite their visual resemblance.
