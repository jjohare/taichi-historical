# 52. Apparent Close Up

Historical image study. Modern teaching may differ as the art evolves. Names and numbers are preserved from [moves.md](../../moves.md); notes are editorial observations and interpretations, not a reconstruction of every historical instruction.

## Identification

**direct** — Second Apparent Close Up.

## Position

Both open hands finish in front after the preceding fist action.

## Transition

Compare the gathering phase and renewed forward extension in Chen's sequence.

Preceded by: Step Forward, Parry Block and Punch. Followed by: Cross Hands.

## Application study — interpretation

Releasing or changing an existing contact provides one possible study of the withdrawal and return.

## Breathing and attention — editorial suggestions

Breathing and attention are editorial practice suggestions, not recovered instructions for this particular photograph. Allow an easy, unforced breath; do not impose a fixed inhalation or exhalation on each numbered movement.

Keep attention through the retreat as well as the forward phase.

## Name and context

Apparent Close Up occurs at section endings but is followed by further movement.
