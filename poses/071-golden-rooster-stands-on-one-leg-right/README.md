# 71. Golden Rooster Stands on One Leg, Right

Historical image study. Modern teaching may differ as the art evolves. Names and numbers are preserved from [moves.md](../../moves.md); notes are editorial observations and interpretations, not a reconstruction of every historical instruction.

## Identification

**direct-with-naming-caveat** — Standing on the right leg, left knee raised. The 1931 heading calls this left. The 1933 webpage URL ends in photo-185 despite caption 186; do not infer side from the filename.

## Position

The right leg supports the body while the left knee and corresponding hand rise.

## Transition

The support changes sides before the sequence returns to retreating steps.

Preceded by: Golden Rooster Stands on One Leg, Left. Followed by: Step Back and Repulse the Monkey, Left.

## Application study — interpretation

Study the coordination of the lifted knee and hand as a relationship rather than an isolated high knee.

## Breathing and attention — editorial suggestions

Breathing and attention are editorial practice suggestions, not recovered instructions for this particular photograph. Allow an easy, unforced breath; do not impose a fixed inhalation or exhalation on each numbered movement.

Notice whether changing support produces unnecessary breath-holding.

## Name and context

Chen's caption and image filename disagree in this pair; the catalogue follows the caption and visible leg position.
