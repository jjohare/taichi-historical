# 16. Embrace the Tiger and Return to Mountain

Historical image study. Modern teaching may differ as the art evolves. Names and numbers are preserved from [moves.md](../../moves.md); notes are editorial observations and interpretations, not a reconstruction of every historical instruction.

## Identification

**direct** — The turning action and rollback, press and push all belong to this compound entry; do not invent extra move numbers for its four panels.

## Position

A turn leads into a sequence that includes rollback, press and push; the montage sometimes gives several panels.

## Transition

Treat this as a compound passage rather than assigning a new movement number to every frame.

Preceded by: Cross Hands. Followed by: Fist Under Elbow.

## Application study — interpretation

Study receiving from a changed direction and recovering a forward line of contact.

## Breathing and attention — editorial suggestions

Breathing and attention are editorial practice suggestions, not recovered instructions for this particular photograph. Allow an easy, unforced breath; do not impose a fixed inhalation or exhalation on each numbered movement.

Keep the turn connected to the feet instead of twisting the shoulders alone.

## Name and context

The tiger and mountain wording is preserved as a traditional title, without an asserted origin legend.
