# 97. Turn Body and Swing Over Lotus

Historical image study. Modern teaching may differ as the art evolves. Names and numbers are preserved from [moves.md](../../moves.md); notes are editorial observations and interpretations, not a reconstruction of every historical instruction.

## Identification

**direct** — The lifted leg and hands indicate the lotus-kick phase; a single frame does not show the complete circular path.

## Position

The leg is lifted with the hands gathered near its path.

## Transition

The photograph captures a phase of the circular kick, not its complete sweep or landing.

Preceded by: Step back and Ride the Tiger. Followed by: Bend the Bow and Shoot the Tiger.

## Application study — interpretation

A sweeping leg action with coordinated hands is a possible reading; the still cannot specify timing.

## Breathing and attention — editorial suggestions

Breathing and attention are editorial practice suggestions, not recovered instructions for this particular photograph. Allow an easy, unforced breath; do not impose a fixed inhalation or exhalation on each numbered movement.

Follow balance and orientation rather than trying to reproduce a high kick from a photograph.

## Name and context

Lotus supplies the movement's title; a flower analogy does not establish an exact trajectory.
