# 37. Left Separation Kick

Historical image study. Modern teaching may differ as the art evolves. Names and numbers are preserved from [moves.md](../../moves.md); notes are editorial observations and interpretations, not a reconstruction of every historical instruction.

## Identification

**direct** — Preparation and left separation kick are separate panels.

## Position

The left leg lifts and extends while the hands open apart.

## Transition

Compare the landing and reorganisation from the preceding right kick before the left leg rises.

Preceded by: Right Separation Kick. Followed by: Turn Body and Left Heel Kick.

## Application study — interpretation

Study how upper-body contact and a free leg might coexist without leaning to reach.

## Breathing and attention — editorial suggestions

Breathing and attention are editorial practice suggestions, not recovered instructions for this particular photograph. Allow an easy, unforced breath; do not impose a fixed inhalation or exhalation on each numbered movement.

Let attention remain on the standing foot while the other leg moves.

## Name and context

The separate preparation image helps prevent a lifted-knee frame being mistaken for the finished kick.
