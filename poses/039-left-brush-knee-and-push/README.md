# 39. Left Brush Knee and Push

Historical image study. Modern teaching may differ as the art evolves. Names and numbers are preserved from [moves.md](../../moves.md); notes are editorial observations and interpretations, not a reconstruction of every historical instruction.

## Identification

**direct** — Left Brush Knee after the turning kick; camera-relative direction differs from the opening section.

## Position

The left hand is low beside the leading knee while the opposite palm reaches forward.

## Transition

Compare the front foot placement and trunk direction before attending to the reaching hand.

Preceded by: Turn Body and Left Heel Kick. Followed by: Right Brush Knee and Push.

## Application study — interpretation

Study simultaneous clearing with one hand and forward contact with the other; no single target is prescribed here.

## Breathing and attention — editorial suggestions

Breathing and attention are editorial practice suggestions, not recovered instructions for this particular photograph. Allow an easy, unforced breath; do not impose a fixed inhalation or exhalation on each numbered movement.

Let the forward hand and the change of weight remain part of one action.

## Name and context

Left names the side of the brushing action in this catalogue.
