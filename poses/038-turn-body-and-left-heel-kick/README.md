# 38. Turn Body and Left Heel Kick

Historical image study. Modern teaching may differ as the art evolves. Names and numbers are preserved from [moves.md](../../moves.md); notes are editorial observations and interpretations, not a reconstruction of every historical instruction.

## Identification

**direct** — Turning left heel kick; back view is part of the sequence, not an image flip.

## Position

The left leg extends with the heel presented; the body may face away from the camera after a turn.

## Transition

The still captures the extended leg, not the complete turning or landing path.

Preceded by: Left Separation Kick. Followed by: Left Brush Knee and Push.

## Application study — interpretation

A heel-led pushing action is an interpretive reading of the foot shape.

## Breathing and attention — editorial suggestions

Breathing and attention are editorial practice suggestions, not recovered instructions for this particular photograph. Allow an easy, unforced breath; do not impose a fixed inhalation or exhalation on each numbered movement.

Keep breathing easy while balancing; do not use breath-holding to maintain the pose.

## Name and context

Left identifies the kicking leg here, unlike the standing-leg convention used for Golden Rooster.
