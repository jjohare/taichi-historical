# 90. Cross Kick

Historical image study. Modern teaching may differ as the art evolves. Names and numbers are preserved from [moves.md](../../moves.md); notes are editorial observations and interpretations, not a reconstruction of every historical instruction.

## Identification

**direct-with-variant** — The 1931 source describes an instep/slapping cross-body action; Chen caption 230 explicitly calls his image a pressing kick. Keep the Chen example as a variant, not an exact mechanical equivalent.

## Position

The raised leg crosses the body's line while the upper body organises a corresponding hand action.

## Transition

A single image cannot establish the complete leg path. Yang and Chen show related but differing versions.

Preceded by: High Pat on On Horse with Palm Thrust. Followed by: Step Forward and Punch Groin.

## Application study — interpretation

Compare an instep or slapping action with Chen's pressing-kick description; do not collapse them into one technique.

## Breathing and attention — editorial suggestions

Breathing and attention are editorial practice suggestions, not recovered instructions for this particular photograph. Allow an easy, unforced breath; do not impose a fixed inhalation or exhalation on each numbered movement.

Keep awareness of the supporting foot during the crossing action.

## Name and context

This is explicitly catalogued as a historical variant comparison, not an exact match between manuals.
