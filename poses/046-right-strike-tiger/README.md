# 46. Right Strike Tiger

Historical image study. Modern teaching may differ as the art evolves. Names and numbers are preserved from [moves.md](../../moves.md); notes are editorial observations and interpretations, not a reconstruction of every historical instruction.

## Identification

**direct** — Right Strike Tiger, with the upper fist beside the head.

## Position

One fist is high beside the head while the other occupies a lower line in the opposite orientation.

## Transition

Read the shift from the preceding left Tiger before the next heel kick.

Preceded by: Left Strike Tiger. Followed by: Turn Body and Right Heel Kick.

## Application study — interpretation

Study the relationship of upper cover and lower contact as an editorial interpretation.

## Breathing and attention — editorial suggestions

Breathing and attention are editorial practice suggestions, not recovered instructions for this particular photograph. Allow an easy, unforced breath; do not impose a fixed inhalation or exhalation on each numbered movement.

Avoid drawing the high shoulder up towards the ear.

## Name and context

The paired left and right entries are retained separately, including their differing camera views.
