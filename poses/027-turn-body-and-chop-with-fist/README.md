# 27. Turn Body and Chop with Fist

Historical image study. Modern teaching may differ as the art evolves. Names and numbers are preserved from [moves.md](../../moves.md); notes are editorial observations and interpretations, not a reconstruction of every historical instruction.

## Identification

**direct** — Two phases of the turning punch; the change of view follows the turn.

## Position

Two plates show a turning action and a fist-led continuation.

## Transition

Compare the before-and-after body directions; a single frame cannot recover the full turn.

Preceded by: Fan Through the Back. Followed by: Step Forward, Parry Block and Punch.

## Application study — interpretation

A turning deflection followed by contact is a possible study of the sequence.

## Breathing and attention — editorial suggestions

Breathing and attention are editorial practice suggestions, not recovered instructions for this particular photograph. Allow an easy, unforced breath; do not impose a fixed inhalation or exhalation on each numbered movement.

Keep the gaze and feet involved in the turn rather than forcing the neck.

## Name and context

Chen names a corresponding later passage differently; those images are not silently treated as identical.
