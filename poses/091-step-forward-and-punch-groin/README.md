# 91. Step Forward and Punch Groin

Historical image study. Modern teaching may differ as the art evolves. Names and numbers are preserved from [moves.md](../../moves.md); notes are editorial observations and interpretations, not a reconstruction of every historical instruction.

## Identification

**direct** — Low forward punch after Cross Kick; sequence separates it from the earlier downward punch.

## Position

A low forward fist follows the kick, with the body directed along the stepping line.

## Transition

Its late placement after Cross Kick distinguishes it from the earlier downward punch.

Preceded by: Cross Kick. Followed by: Step Forward and Grasp the Sparrow's tail.

## Application study — interpretation

The supplied title names a low target; the image alone establishes only the solo position.

## Breathing and attention — editorial suggestions

Breathing and attention are editorial practice suggestions, not recovered instructions for this particular photograph. Allow an easy, unforced breath; do not impose a fixed inhalation or exhalation on each numbered movement.

Keep attention on the path into the stance rather than only the final fist.

## Name and context

Punch Groin is retained as the supplied name, without turning a solo archival image into partner instruction.
