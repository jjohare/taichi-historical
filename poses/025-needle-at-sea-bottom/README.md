# 25. Needle at Sea Bottom

Historical image study. Modern teaching may differ as the art evolves. Names and numbers are preserved from [moves.md](../../moves.md); notes are editorial observations and interpretations, not a reconstruction of every historical instruction.

## Identification

**direct** — The body folds forward and the leading hand points down.

## Position

The trunk inclines and one hand reaches down towards the floor.

## Transition

Compare the lowered reach with the following upward and forward opening of Fan Through the Back.

Preceded by: Left Brush Knee and Push. Followed by: Fan Through the Back.

## Application study — interpretation

A downward redirection is a possible study theme; depth in an old photograph is not a required modern range.

## Breathing and attention — editorial suggestions

Breathing and attention are editorial practice suggestions, not recovered instructions for this particular photograph. Allow an easy, unforced breath; do not impose a fixed inhalation or exhalation on each numbered movement.

Keep attention on the supporting legs while the hand lowers.

## Name and context

Sea-bottom imagery names the downward reach; no literal depth or origin story is implied.
