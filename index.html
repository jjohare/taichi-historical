楊式太極拳

A historical record.
An evolving practice.

This archive studies the form as documented in the images and manuals of their time. Modern teaching may differ as the art continues to evolve. These records offer historical context, rather than a standard against which every present-day school should be judged.

The 1931 Yang Chengfu manual and Chen Weiming’s 1933 supplement are read alongside a 2010 drawing montage. Our position descriptions, application ideas and practice cues are editorial interpretations; differences and uncertain matches stay visible.

    PROVENANCE & METHOD

    Read the sources.
    Keep the differences.

    This is a Yang-lineage archive. Chen’s supplement explicitly compares his photographs with Yang Chengfu’s. A similar silhouette alone is not evidence of Chee Soo’s Lee style.

    The supplied list starts at 0 and skips 55. Its named sequence aligns with the Yang Family 103-form worksheet; the original numbering is retained here. Images expand some compound actions and compress repeated ones, so the counts need not agree.
